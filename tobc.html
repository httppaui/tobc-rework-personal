Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers before you book.

What documents do I need to book?
Typically a valid Seaman's Book (SRB), government-issued ID, and any prerequisite certificates. Requirements vary per course — check the course page for the full list.
How do I get a refund if I cancel?
Cancellations 7+ days before: full refund. 3–6 days: 50% refund. Within 48 hours: no refund. See our full Cancellation Policy for details.
Can a manning agency book for multiple crew?
Yes! Manning agencies have a dedicated portal with bulk booking, compliance tracking, and consolidated invoicing. Register as an Agency to access these features.
Are all training centers MARINA-accredited?
Yes. TOBC only lists MARINA-accredited training centers. All courses meet STCW and local maritime regulation standards.

About TOBC

The Online Booking Corp. — a maritime training marketplace empowering seafarers, agencies, and training providers across the Philippines.

Our Platform

Our platform offers a wide range of training services across various industries and professions. Whether you are a professional looking to upskill, a student seeking supplementary education, or a training provider looking to showcase your expertise, TOBC provides a marketplace where these connections are made possible.

We understand the importance of continuous learning and professional development, and we are committed to making the process of finding, booking, and providing training services as effortless as possible.

Mission & Vision

What Drives Us

Our Mission

To empower individuals and organizations by providing a seamless and efficient platform for booking and offering training services — making continuous learning effortless and accessible.

Our Vision

To be a leading global platform that fosters growth and development by connecting individuals and training providers — creating a learning ecosystem where quality training is easily discoverable.

Our Story

At TOBC, we are passionate about education and training. We are a dedicated team of professionals who believe in the transformative power of learning, coming together to build a platform that simplifies maritime training access.

Our Values

Integrity, accessibility, and excellence guide everything we do. We partner only with MARINA-accredited centers to ensure every course on our platform meets the highest maritime training standards.
